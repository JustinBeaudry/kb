# Refine the vault

Use the [KB entry point](../skills/kb/SKILL.md) and the refine section of the
[maintenance guide](../skills/kb/references/maintenance.md).

Establish findings, apply authorized changes, preserve provenance and verify
the resulting content and links. Report wider or ambiguous decisions before
acting, and measure the improvement rather than assuming it.
